# Packlet Defense

A 3D voxel PvP-style arena game: one player summons mythological enemies with cards, the other defends the crystal with heroes.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (internet needed once, to load Three.js).

## Features
- **3D voxel lobby** — walk the hub, click buildings: Pack Shop, Rip Packs, Hero Shop, Tavern (chat), Collection, Arena
- **Pack ripping in public** — tear packs open in front of the lobby crowd; other players gather around and react in chat
- **Two roles** — Attack: spend mana to summon enemies from your equipped cards (upgrade mana max and regen mid-match). Defend: spend gold to place heroes on pads (upgrade gold rate and crystal repair)
- **500-card roster** — 250 unique monster cards + 250 unique hero cards, across five tiers each
- **Card variants** — every card has Normal, Alt Art, Gold, and Rainbow versions with different pull odds and battle bonuses
- **Real card art** — AI-generated voxel-style illustrations in `art/`, one per creature family, with gold/rainbow/alt-art treatments
- **Equip system** — equip your top 8 enemy cards and 8 heroes before battle; new cards you pull unlock new monsters to summon
- **Mythology roster** — Imps, Skeletons, Dire Wolves, Wraiths, Minotaurs, Cave Trolls, Banshees, Fenrir, Golems, Hydras, Phoenixes, Elder Wyrms, Oni, Vampires, Griffins, and 235 more
- **Heroes** — Squire, Archer, Knight, Battle Mage, Frost Druid (slows), Valkyrie, Paladin (aura), Dragon Knight (splash), and 242 more
- **Lobby sim** — tavern chat with other players, challenge list with difficulty ratings
- Progress saves automatically in your browser
