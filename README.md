# Packlet Defense

A 3D voxel multiplayer-style arena game: one player summons mythological enemies with cards, the other defends the crystal with heroes.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (internet needed once, to load Three.js).

## Features
- **3D voxel lobby** — walk the hub, click buildings: Pack Shop, Rip Packs, Hero Shop, Tavern (chat), Collection, Arena
- **Pack ripping** — buy packs with coins, tear them open for Common → Legendary cards
- **Two roles** — Attack: spend mana to summon enemies from your cards. Defend: spend gold to place heroes on pads
- **Mythology roster** — Goblin Imp, Skeleton, Dire Wolf, Wraith, Minotaur, Cave Troll, Banshee, Fenrir, Obsidian Golem, Hydra (splits on death), Phoenix (revives), Elder Wyrm
- **Heroes** — Squire, Archer, Knight, Battle Mage, Frost Druid (slows), Valkyrie, Paladin (aura), Dragon Knight (splash)
- **Lobby sim** — tavern chat with other players, challenge list with difficulty ratings
- Progress saves automatically in your browser
